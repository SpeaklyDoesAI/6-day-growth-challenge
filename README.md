# 6 Day Growth Challenge

A single-file static page for Creative Owners Club's 6 Day Growth Challenge. Visitors pick their level, enter daily signups, and see their estimated challenge bonus and team prize share.

- No backend: everything lives in `index.html`.
- Loads only Google Fonts; progress is saved in the visitor's browser (localStorage).
- Deployed on Render as a free static site (see `render.yaml`).
